Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about the Threads Media Downloader

How do I install the extension?

+

Simply click the "Add to Chrome/Edge" or "Add to Firefox" button above. The extension will be automatically installed and ready to use.

What types of media can I download?

+

You can download photos, videos, and reels from Threads posts. The extension supports various image formats (JPG, PNG, WebP) and video formats.

Is my data safe?

+

Yes, absolutely. The extension is privacy-first and doesn't collect, store, or share any of your personal information. All processing happens locally on your device.

Does it work with private posts?

+

The extension can only download media from posts that are visible to you on Threads. It cannot access private or restricted content.

Can I download multiple files at once?

+

Yes! The extension supports batch downloading. You can select multiple posts or media files and download them all simultaneously.

What browsers are supported?

+

The extension works on Google Chrome, Mozilla Firefox, Microsoft Edge, Brave, and other Chromium-based browsers.

How do I report bugs or request features?

+

You can contact us at maxdemon@outlook.com to report bugs, request features, or get support. We appreciate your feedback!

Is the extension free to use?

+

Yes, the extension is completely free and open source. It's licensed under GPLv3, ensuring it remains free forever.
